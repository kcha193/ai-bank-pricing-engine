# Phase 4a source register

Evidence checked on 21 November 2026 NZT. The public sources below provide
definitions, market context and checks on modelling scope. The Phase 4a
baseline and sensitivity inputs come from the repository and illustrative
scenarios. The evidence review applied no economic calibration.

### Reporting comparisons and calibration limits

- ANZ reported NZ$58m of Stage 1 loss allowance on NZ$113,522m of Stage 1
  gross residential mortgage loans and advances. The derived allowance
  density is about **5.1 bp**. Across all stages the comparable balance
  sheet ratio is NZ$204m / NZ$119,340m, about **17.1 bp**, and mixes 12-month
  and lifetime loss horizons. Both calculations exclude off-balance-sheet
  amounts. The Stage 1 ratio can serve as a directional reasonableness
  check once eligibility, timing and exposure basis are aligned. It cannot
  substantiate a specific multiplier for prime originations.
  [ANZ B3, p. 86](https://www.anz.co.nz/content/dam/anzconz/documents/about-us/disclosure-statements/ANZ-Bank-NZ-Ltd-DS-Sep26.pdf)
- A period impairment charge reflects changes in accounting allowances,
  recoveries and other adjustments. NPL and arrears ratios are distress
  stocks. Gross default incidence measures frequency and excludes loss
  severity. Regulatory IRB estimates answer a capital question. Each
  comparison needs its own horizon, numerator, denominator and population.
- The audit's prime-mortgage comparison remains a hypothesis. Calibration
  requires approved multi-vintage NZ mortgage outcome data with default
  events, drawn and committed exposures, recoveries, collateral values,
  valuation dates, LVR, product and risk grade. Document selection, cures,
  prepayment, recovery lags and sampling uncertainty before fitting.
  [November audit, FIN-001](../2026-11-13-full-repository-audit.md#fin-001-canonical-recommendation-breaches-its-policy-band-driven-by-uncalibrated-inputs)

A closing stock cannot identify acquisition flow. A calibration must reconcile
opening stock, matured balances, renewals, switches, gross new money, runoff and
closing stock over a stated window. Estimating reachable share, participation,
conversion, concentration and marginal acquisition cost requires authorised
offers, booking, maturity, retention and source-of-funds outcomes by term,
channel, rate and balance tier. Identify internal transfers from cheaper
products before counting net incremental funding.

The November 2026 FSR uses the 1-year swap rate for its 1-year new mortgage
spread and a 6-month bank bill reference for its 6-month term-deposit
comparison. These are comparison methodologies. For a 12-month term deposit,
select and validate its own repricing benchmark, behaviour assumptions and
marginal funding attribution. Bank-specific wholesale execution, cross-currency
basis where relevant, term liquidity premium, buffer costs and behavioural
duration still need Treasury/ALM evidence.
[November 2026 Financial Stability Report, figures 3.12–3.14](https://www.rbnz.govt.nz/financial-stability/financial-stability-report/financial-stability-reports/2026/nov/financial-stability-report-november-2026/web-version)

Capital/Finance must agree the legal entity and applicable conditions, the
capital definition and effective version, the exposure and RWA basis, the
portfolio allocation and hurdle. Validate any regulatory proxy against an
authorised reference calculation. Size economic capital for unexpected loss
under an approved risk measure and horizon, with documented diversification
and allocation. Public prudential rules alone cannot justify that choice.
